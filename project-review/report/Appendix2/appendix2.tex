\noindent{\Huge\bfseries APPENDIX B}\\[0.3cm]
{\Large\bfseries COMPLEX ENGINEERING PROBLEM}\\[0.3cm]

A problem qualifies as a complex engineering problem when characteristic WP1 is present and at least two more of WP2 to WP7 are also present. This project shows all seven.

{\small\setstretch{1.05}
\begin{longtable}{|p{2.6cm}|p{9.2cm}|p{2.4cm}|}
\hline \textbf{Characteristic} & \textbf{How it shows in the project} & \textbf{Chapters} \\ \hline
\endhead
WP1 Depth of knowledge (compulsory) & Transformers (DistilBERT), speech recognition (Whisper), voice anti-spoofing (wav2vec2), boosted trees (XGBoost) with exact feature attribution, e-mail authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), and bootstrap statistics. These go beyond routine coursework. & 2, 4, 5 \\ \hline
WP2 Conflicting requirements & Catch rate against false alarms against speed against privacy. For example, a public-only SMS vocabulary costs 0.010 of macro-F1 but keeps private text off the phone, and the behaviour gate raises spam recall from 0.611 to 0.912 at the cost of 8.5\% of cleared legitimate mail sent to Suspicious. & 5, 8 \\ \hline
WP3 Depth of analysis & No ready-made three-channel tool exists. SVM, naive Bayes, logistic regression, MobileBERT and an isolation forest were compared before the design was chosen, and each choice is justified by a measurement. & 5, 8 \\ \hline
WP4 Familiarity of issues & The literature survey shows no earlier system that joins the three channels with sender behaviour. & 1, 2 \\ \hline
WP5 Codes and standards & SPF, DKIM and DMARC (RFC 7208, RFC 6376, RFC 7489), the TRAI sender registry rules (TCCCPR 2018) and the DPDP Act 2023. & 3, 4, 5 \\ \hline
WP6 Stakeholders & Users, banks, the telecom regulator and mail providers. Privacy needs led to the on-phone SMS check, and the regulator's rules led to the sender and caller-number checks. & 1, 3 \\ \hline
WP7 Interdependence & The model service, website, phone app and database depend on each other and were tested together: phone and server parity tests, and a Gmail end-to-end test. & 4, 7 \\ \hline
\end{longtable}
}

\noindent\textbf{Total WPs present (including WP1): 7. Qualifies as a complex engineering problem: Yes.}
